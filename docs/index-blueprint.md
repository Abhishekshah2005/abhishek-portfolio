# THE INDEX — build blueprint ("Problems, Solved")
Approved concept. This is the concrete content + system to build. Placeholders marked ⛔ swap for real figures later.

## Message
"I solve business problems with finance, software and AI." Problem-first: the visitor scans, recognises their pain, opens it, sees the fix + proof, books.

## Page structure (one continuous, restrained page)
1. **Masthead** — wordmark left · "Contact" right.
2. **Statement** — one line: *"I fix what slows businesses down — with finance, software & AI."* + a thin sub: *Accounting · CFO · Automation · AI · Software — UK · UAE · India.*
3. **THE INDEX** — the hero: a numbered list of named problems in three quiet groups, each line tagged by discipline, each opening (FLIP) to a one-line solution + a proof figure + a "how" link. A discipline filter (All · Finance · Software · AI) reflows it.
4. **Invitation** — *"Recognise any of these? Let's solve yours."* → Request a consultation.

## The Index content (12 problems, 3 groups)
**See clearly** — visibility & trust
1. "You can't see cash coming." — `Finance` — Real-time cash-flow + forecasting on Xero/Sage. ⛔*13-week runway, always visible.*
2. "Your books span three countries." — `Finance` — UK · UAE · India accounting & compliance, one clean close. ⛔*Month-end in days, not weeks.*
3. "You're flying without a CFO." — `Finance` — Fractional CFO: P&L strategy, projections, board-ready reporting. ⛔*Investor-ready in 30 days.*
4. "Audit season is chaos." — `Finance` — Audit-ready systems & controls. ⛔*Clean audit, no scramble.*

**Run leaner** — automation & AI
5. "Your team drowns in manual work." — `Software` `AI` — Automations & integrations for repetitive ops. ⛔*N hours/week reclaimed.*
6. "Data lives in ten disconnected tools." — `Software` — Integrations + a single source of truth. ⛔*One system, all your data.*
7. "You answer the same questions all day." — `AI` — AI agents/assistants for support & ops. ⛔*24/7 answers, leaner headcount.*
8. "Your call centre can't keep up." — `AI` `Business` — Call-centre ops design + AI assist. ⛔*Faster response, lower cost.*

**Scale confidently** — build & systems
9. "Spreadsheets are holding you back." — `Software` — Custom software/SaaS to replace fragile sheets. ⛔*A system you can grow on.*
10. "You need an app or site that performs." — `Software` — Apps & high-performance websites. ⛔*Shipped fast, built to convert.*
11. "Sales is ad-hoc, not a system." — `Business` `Software` — CRM + business-development systems. ⛔*A repeatable pipeline.*

## Visual system
- **Palette:** warm paper + ink + **one accent** — recommended **deep signal green `#1F6B4B`** (money/growth; distinct from every prior attempt). Alt: ink navy.
- **Type:** strong **grotesk** (Geist) for index lines (large, tight) + **mono** for numbers/tags/figures. *(No serif — a clean, confident, non-editorial register.)*
- **Motion:** hairline separators draw in; lines lift magnetically on hover with the number + tags illuminating; open = FLIP (siblings slide aside, solution+proof reveal); figures count on open; filter reflows with FLIP. Restrained, purposeful.
- **Scroll:** scan the index; a sticky tally ("12 problems, solved"); groups announce themselves as you pass.
- **Cursor:** native (restraint). Magnetic on the filter + CTA.
