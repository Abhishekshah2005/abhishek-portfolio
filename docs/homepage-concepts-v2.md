# Abhishek Shah — Homepage Concepts v2 (8 directions)
### Concept development only · no code until one is approved
**The one message:** *"I solve business problems using finance, software and AI."*
**Guardrails:** original — NOT generic SaaS, NOT an editorial template, NOT an Apple clone; and not any previously-rejected direction (cyberpunk/OS, AI-network, object hero, storytelling engine, "The Ledger" editorial). Almost-monochrome + one accent. Lead-gen for high-value clients.

**The insight that shapes all 8:** high-value clients don't buy "services" — they buy *the end of a problem they already feel*. So the strongest concepts lead with the **client's problem/outcome**, then reveal that finance + software + AI is how he solves it, then prove it. Concepts are ranked on: on-message · originality · lead-gen · fits his tri-disciplinary background · buildable-to-premium.

---

## 1 — THE INDEX · "Problems, Solved"
- **Core idea.** The homepage *is* a large, confident index of the business problems he solves ("Cash you can't see coming." "Books across three countries." "A team drowning in manual work." "No system to scale on."). Each line opens to reveal the finance+software+AI approach and a proof figure.
- **Emotional goal.** Recognition — *"that's exactly my problem"* — then confidence.
- **Layout.** Full-bleed numbered index, hairline separators, tiny discipline tags `[Finance] [Software] [AI]` per line; opening a line pushes the rest aside (FLIP) to show a tight solution + a metric + a "how" link. Index left, detail right.
- **Motion.** FLIP expand/collapse; lines lift magnetically on hover; tags illuminate; figures count.
- **Scroll.** Scanning the index; a sticky tally ("N problems solved"); a discipline filter reflows it.
- **Palette.** Paper + ink; ONE accent (deep signal green = money/growth) only on the active line + tags.
- **Typography.** Strong grotesk for the index lines (large, tight) + mono for tags/figures. (No serif → distinct from the rejected editorial route.)
- **Interaction.** Hover lift; click to expand solution+proof; filter by Finance/Software/AI (proves range).
- **Why it generates leads.** Every line is a pain hook the visitor owns; expansion delivers solution→proof→CTA; the page self-qualifies.
- **Why it suits his background.** Frames accounting/CFO + AI/automation/CRM/SaaS/apps as *answers to named problems*; the tags make the rare combination legible at a glance.

## 2 — BEFORE / AFTER · "From friction to flow"
- **Core idea.** A living before→after. Opens on a business in friction (scattered figures, manual chaos), and as you scroll the same scene resolves into clarity and flow. He is the transformation.
- **Emotional goal.** Relief + aspiration — *"I want that after."*
- **Layout.** A morphing scene (or split with a seam); "before" annotations reorganise into an ordered "after."
- **Motion.** Clip-path/FLIP reorganisation from messy → aligned; colour shifts tense → calm.
- **Scroll.** Scroll = the transformation timeline; optional draggable before/after seam.
- **Palette.** Before = desaturated/tense; after = calm paper + one accent. The palette itself transforms.
- **Typography.** Grotesk + annotation labels.
- **Interaction.** Drag the seam; hover annotations.
- **Why leads.** Shows the *outcome* viscerally; CTA "get your after."
- **Why it suits him.** Finance brings order, software/AI remove the chaos — the "after" is literally his work.

## 3 — THE BRIEF · "It starts with your problem"
- **Core idea.** A consultative, question-led page that poses the visitor's likely problems ("Where is your margin leaking?" "Can you close the books across UK, UAE & India in days, not weeks?") and answers each with his approach + proof.
- **Emotional goal.** Feeling understood; bespoke attention.
- **Layout.** One question at a time, large; the answer reveals beneath as capability + proof; unfolds like a real brief.
- **Motion.** Question swaps (mask/scramble-free), answers reveal, a quiet "brief" progress.
- **Scroll.** Each scroll = next question→answer; a consultation unfolding.
- **Palette.** Calm trustworthy monochrome + one accent.
- **Typography.** Grotesk questions, mono meta.
- **Interaction.** Visitor selects which questions apply (chips) → tailors the answers; ends "book the real conversation."
- **Why leads.** Literally qualifies by problem → high-intent enquiries.
- **Why it suits him.** Advisor-who-builds; questions span every discipline.

## 4 — PROOF LEDGER · "The record"
- **Core idea.** Results-first: a running ledger of quantified wins (hours automated, £ recovered, runway added, systems shipped, 3-country closes). The person sits behind the proof.
- **Emotional goal.** Trust through evidence.
- **Layout.** A tally/statement of metrics as the hero; each expands to a mini-case (problem→action→result).
- **Motion.** Numbers count on view; rows reveal; a "bottom line" totals.
- **Scroll.** Scroll tallies the record.
- **Palette.** Monochrome + accent (green positives).
- **Typography.** Tabular mono numerals prominent + grotesk labels.
- **Interaction.** Hover a figure → the story; filter by discipline/industry.
- **Why leads.** Proof is the #1 converter for serious buyers; CTA "add your business to the record."
- **Why it suits him.** Finance-native (a ledger), quantifies AI/software impact. **Risk:** needs real numbers; can read dry without craft.

## 5 — ASK · "Tell me what's slowing you down"
- **Core idea.** One elegant prompt: the visitor types their problem; the site responds with how he'd approach it (curated/scripted mapping now, real AI later). AI-native, bespoke.
- **Emotional goal.** Intrigue + agency — *"he gets me."*
- **Layout.** Near-empty; one refined input line; responses compose beneath as tailored capability + proof + CTA.
- **Motion.** Thoughtful text choreography (never chatbot-spammy).
- **Scroll.** Minimal until engaged; response grows the page.
- **Palette.** Quiet monochrome + accent.
- **Typography.** Grotesk + mono.
- **Interaction.** The input + smart suggestions = a genuinely useful mini-diagnostic; capture email to "send the full plan."
- **Why leads.** Captures intent + contact; highly memorable.
- **Why it suits him.** Demonstrates AI *by being AI-assisted*; responses show finance+software thinking. **Risk:** gimmick if shallow; needs response logic; the empty state must still sell.

## 6 — THREE LENSES · "One problem, three lenses"
- **Core idea.** Take one business situation and view it through three lenses — Finance, Software, AI — each re-rendering the composition to show how he'd solve it that way, then a "combined" state showing integration.
- **Emotional goal.** *"He sees my business from every angle."*
- **Layout.** A central subject + a lens switch; switching morphs annotations/visuals; a fourth combined view.
- **Motion.** Lens-switch morph (shared elements re-annotate), FLIP.
- **Scroll.** Deepens each lens / moves subject → lenses → combined.
- **Palette.** One base + a whisper tint per lens.
- **Typography.** Grotesk.
- **Interaction.** The lens switcher (the core toy).
- **Why leads.** Proves range + integration; CTA "see your business through all three."
- **Why it suits him.** The most *literal* embodiment of "finance, software and AI." **Risk:** more about him than the client's problem; the subject can feel abstract.

## 7 — THE DIAGNOSIS · "A specialist for business problems"
- **Core idea.** Positions him like a top specialist: symptom → root-cause diagnosis (finance) → prescription + build (software/AI) → outcome.
- **Emotional goal.** Reassurance; safe hands.
- **Layout.** Symptom → diagnosis → treatment → outcome flow; calm, precise.
- **Motion.** Measured, exact reveals.
- **Scroll.** A case unfolds like a diagnosis.
- **Palette.** Calm neutrals + one accent.
- **Typography.** Grotesk.
- **Interaction.** Pick a symptom → see the path.
- **Why leads.** Consultative, high trust; CTA "book a diagnosis."
- **Why it suits him.** Advisor framing across disciplines. **Risk:** overlaps The Brief; clinical metaphor can feel cold.

## 8 — THE P&L · "Your business, in the black"
- **Core idea.** The homepage as a living financial statement where line items are his capabilities and their *impact*, tallying to a bottom line: growth.
- **Emotional goal.** *"He speaks my numbers."*
- **Layout.** A reimagined statement (impact columns); scroll tallies to the bottom line.
- **Motion.** Figures tally; rows reveal; the bottom line resolves.
- **Scroll.** Reading down the statement to the result.
- **Palette.** Monochrome + green/red finance semantics, sparingly.
- **Typography.** Tabular mono numerals + grotesk.
- **Interaction.** Hover a line → what it means for you.
- **Why leads.** Reframes services as ROI; CTA "improve your bottom line."
- **Why it suits him.** Deeply finance-native; casts AI/software as line items that move the P&L. **Risk:** can read niche/spreadsheet-y; must stay warm.

## Recommendation — **Concept 1: THE INDEX ("Problems, Solved")**, fusing the best of #6, #4, #2
It is the only direction that nails all five criteria at once, and it *is* the message: a homepage that leads with the **problems he solves**, tagged by discipline so the finance+software+AI combination is instantly legible, each opening to a **proof figure**. It's original (an index as a homepage — not SaaS, not editorial, not Apple), confident, deeply lead-generative (every line is a hook the buyer recognises), scalable into a full portfolio (each line → a case), and very buildable to a premium bar with our stack (FLIP, mask reveals, magnetic hover, count-ups).

**It absorbs the runners-up:** the `[Finance][Software][AI]` tags + filter deliver **Three Lenses**; the expanded proof figure delivers **Proof Ledger**; a small before→after micro-visual inside an opened line can deliver **Before/After** — without diluting the core.

**Shape (for when approved):** a masthead; a one-line statement of what he does; then THE INDEX of ~8–12 named problems (grouped: *See clearly · Run leaner · Scale confidently*), each with discipline tags; open one → solution + proof + "how"; a discipline filter; a closing invitation. Almost-monochrome + one accent (deep signal green or ink), grotesk + mono, restrained purposeful motion.

**Dependency:** the problem statements can be written now (from his background); real **proof figures + case detail** should come from him — I'll use honest placeholders until then.
